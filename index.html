What I do

Too many tools, too many places for data.

Many manufacturers run several CAD packages and more than one PDM or PLM system side by side, with network drives filling the gaps. That setup is expensive to maintain and hard to govern. Engineering leaders want to standardize but rarely have the time or in-house expertise to plan it. That's the gap I fill.

01

Engineering systems audit

An inventory of the CAD, FEA, PDM, and PLM tools you use, who uses them, where their data is stored, and how they're configured.

02

Process improvement

A review of your engineering business processes, such as release, change, and data handoffs, with specific opportunities to improve them.

03

Architecture recommendation

A target-state design for your engineering software environment, plus a realistic path to reach it.

04

Transformation & implementation

Hands-on help consolidating tools, migrating data, and delivering the new architecture, followed by ongoing support if you want it.
